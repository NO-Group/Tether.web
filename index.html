Features

Everything You Need,
Nothing You Don't

Tether is built from the ground up with security, speed, and simplicity at its core. Every feature is designed to keep your connections unbreakable.

End-to-End Encryption

Every message, call, and file is encrypted with military-grade protocols. Not even we can read your conversations.

Instant Tethering

Connect with anyone in milliseconds. Our proprietary tethering protocol ensures zero-lag message delivery worldwide.

Group Chats That Feel Alive

Create vibrant groups with up to 10,000 members. Rich reactions, polls, threads, and shared media bring conversations to life.

Voice & Video Calls

Crystal-clear HD voice and video calls with up to 50 participants. Noise cancellation and screen sharing included.

Disappearing Messages

Set messages to self-destruct after a chosen time. From 5 seconds to 7 days — you control the lifespan of every message.

Cross-Platform Sync

Seamlessly switch between phone, tablet, and desktop. Your conversations, media, and settings sync instantly across all devices.

Zero-Knowledge Privacy

We collect zero personal data. No phone number required. No tracking, no ads, no data mining. Ever.

Lightning Fast

Built on a distributed network of edge servers worldwide. Messages deliver in under 100ms no matter where you are.

Custom Themes

Make Tether yours with fully customizable themes, chat wallpapers, font sizes, and accent colors to match your style.

Unlimited Cloud Storage

Share files up to 2GB each. Photos, videos, documents — everything is stored securely in your encrypted cloud vault.

How It Works

Get Tethered in Seconds

Getting started is effortless. Three simple steps and you're connected to everyone who matters.

1

Download Tether

Get the app from Google Play or the App Store. It's free, lightweight, and installs in seconds on any device.

2

Create Your Identity

Set up your profile with a unique Tether ID. No phone number required — we respect your privacy from day one.

3

Start Tethering

Find friends by Tether ID or invite them via a secure link. Send your first message — encrypted, instant, unbreakable.

4

Stay Connected Forever

Sync across all your devices, join group chats, make HD calls, and share memories — all with zero-knowledge privacy.
