Security & Privacy

Your Privacy is Non-Negotiable

Tether was built from scratch with a privacy-first architecture. Here's how we keep your data safe.

End-to-End Encryption

Every message, voice note, image, video, and file is encrypted using the Signal Protocol with AES-256 encryption. Only you and the intended recipient can read your messages — not even Tether's servers can decrypt them. Keys are generated and stored exclusively on your device.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

We cannot see your messages. We cannot read your contacts. We don't know who you're talking to. Our servers process encrypted blobs — we have zero knowledge of your data. Your metadata is protected with onion-routing-inspired relay networks.

No Phone Number Required

Unlike other messaging apps, Tether doesn't require your phone number to sign up. Create an anonymous Tether ID and start messaging immediately. Your real identity stays yours — we never link your account to personal identifiers.

Disappearing Messages

Set any conversation to auto-delete messages after a chosen time period: 5 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 hour, 1 day, or 7 days. Once expired, messages are permanently erased from all devices with no recovery possible — giving you full control over your digital footprint.

Screenshot Protection

Enable screenshot protection on sensitive conversations. When active, recipients cannot capture screenshots of your messages. If they attempt to, you'll be notified immediately. Works across both Android and iOS platforms.

Open Source Protocol

Our encryption protocol is fully open source and has been independently audited by three leading cybersecurity firms. Transparency is trust — anyone can inspect, verify, and contribute to our security infrastructure.

Messaging

Messaging That Feels Alive

Rich, expressive, and lightning fast. Tether messages go beyond plain text.

Instant Tethering

Our proprietary tethering protocol delivers messages in under 100 milliseconds worldwide. Built on a distributed edge network spanning 60+ global data centers, your messages arrive before you can blink — literally faster than human perception.

Rich Media Sharing

Share photos, videos, GIFs, voice notes, documents, and files up to 2GB each. Media is compressed intelligently to preserve quality while saving bandwidth. Original quality files are always available for download from your encrypted cloud vault.

Reactions & Stickers

Express yourself with inline emoji reactions on any message. Choose from thousands of beautifully designed sticker packs, or create your own custom stickers from your photos. Animated stickers bring conversations to life.

Threaded Replies

Reply to specific messages with inline threads to keep conversations organized. Perfect for group chats where multiple topics unfold simultaneously. Threads collapse neatly so your chat never feels cluttered.

Drafts & Scheduled Messages

Compose messages and save them as drafts, or schedule them to send at a specific date and time. Perfect for birthday wishes, reminders, or communicating across time zones without disturbing anyone's sleep.

Edit & Unsend

Made a typo? Change your mind? Edit sent messages within 48 hours or unsend them entirely. Unsent messages are permanently deleted from all recipients' devices — like they never existed.

Calls & Groups

Calls That Crystal Clear

HD voice and video calls with enterprise-grade reliability, plus group features that scale.

HD Voice Calls

Crystal-clear voice calls powered by the Opus codec at 48kHz sample rate. AI-driven noise cancellation filters out background sounds in real-time. Calls are end-to-end encrypted and work seamlessly on low-bandwidth connections.

HD Video Calls

Full HD 1080p video calling with up to 50 participants simultaneously. Features include virtual backgrounds, screen sharing, picture-in-picture mode, and adaptive bitrate streaming that maintains quality even on unstable connections.

Groups Up to 10,000

Create vibrant communities with up to 10,000 members. Groups feature admin controls, member roles, pinned messages, polls, shared calendars, and a searchable media archive. Every group message is fully encrypted.

Broadcast Channels

Create one-to-many broadcast channels for announcements, newsletters, or content distribution. Channels support unlimited subscribers with rich formatting, scheduled posts, and detailed engagement analytics.

Screen Sharing

Share your screen during video calls for presentations, debugging, or just showing something cool. Supports full-screen sharing, window-specific sharing, and annotation tools for collaborative sessions.

Polls & Surveys

Create interactive polls directly in any chat or group. Support for multiple choice, ranking, and open-ended questions. Results update in real-time with beautiful visualizations and anonymous voting options.

Platform

Your App, Your Way

Tether works everywhere and adapts to your personal style.

Cross-Platform Sync

Seamlessly switch between Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux, and the web. All your conversations, media, contacts, and settings sync instantly across every device. Start a chat on your phone, continue on your laptop — zero friction.

Custom Themes

Choose from dozens of stunning pre-built themes or create your own. Customize accent colors, chat wallpapers, bubble styles, font sizes, and more. Dark mode and AMOLED black mode save battery on OLED displays.

Unlimited Cloud Storage

Every photo, video, and file you share is stored in your personal encrypted cloud vault — forever. Access your entire media history from any device without consuming local storage. Completely encrypted, completely yours.

Lightning Performance

Tether is built with a custom rendering engine optimized for messaging. The app launches in under 500ms, scrolls at 120fps, and uses 40% less battery than competing apps. We obsess over every millisecond.

Smart Notifications

AI-powered notification prioritization learns your patterns and surfaces what matters most. Custom notification sounds per contact, scheduled quiet hours, and granular per-chat settings give you complete control.

Accessibility First

Tether is designed to be usable by everyone. Full screen reader support, high contrast modes, adjustable font sizes, and keyboard navigation ensure that no one is left behind. We conform to WCAG 2.1 AA standards.
