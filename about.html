About Us

The Story Behind Tether

We believe privacy is a fundamental human right and that communication should be free from surveillance, data mining, and compromise.

It Started with a Question

In 2023, a group of engineers, designers, and privacy advocates sat in a small apartment in Lagos and asked a simple question: "Why does messaging have to compromise your privacy?"

Every major messaging platform at the time collected data, sold metadata to advertisers, or required your phone number just to sign up. We believed there had to be a better way — a way to stay connected without sacrificing the fundamental right to privacy.

That question became an obsession. And that obsession became Tether.

Built from Scratch

We didn't fork an existing project or license someone else's encryption. We built Tether from the ground up — every protocol, every algorithm, every line of code — with privacy as the foundational layer, not an afterthought.

Our encryption protocol was designed in collaboration with leading cryptographers and has been independently audited three times. We created a proprietary message delivery system that can route encrypted packets across our global network in under 100 milliseconds.

18 months of development. Over 400,000 lines of code. Zero compromises.

Growing a Movement

When we launched Tether in early 2024, we hoped a few thousand people might care about privacy enough to try a new app. We were wrong — millions cared.

Within the first year, Tether grew to 5 million users through word of mouth alone. No paid advertising. No growth hacks. Just people telling other people about an app that genuinely respected their privacy.

Today, over 10 million users across 140+ countries trust Tether for their most important conversations. And we're just getting started.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Every decision we make is guided by these core principles.

Privacy First

Privacy isn't a feature — it's our foundation. Every architectural decision starts with the question: "Does this protect our users' privacy?" If the answer isn't an unequivocal yes, we don't build it.

Radical Transparency

Our encryption protocol is open source. Our security audits are published. Our data practices are documented in plain English. We believe trust is earned through transparency, not marketing.

People Over Profit

We will never sell user data. We will never show ads. We will never monetize your attention. Our business model is simple: build a product so good that people want to support it.

Accessible to All

Secure messaging shouldn't be a luxury. Tether is free to use, works on low-end devices, and is designed to be accessible to people of all abilities, languages, and technical backgrounds.

Relentless Quality

We obsess over every pixel, every millisecond, every interaction. We believe people deserve software that respects their time and delights them with craftsmanship. Good enough is never good enough.

Unbreakable Connections

Our name says it all. We're building technology that strengthens human connections — not technology that exploits them. Every feature exists to bring people closer together, securely.
