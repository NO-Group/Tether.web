Yes, Tether is completely free to download and use. All core features — including messaging, voice calls, video calls, group chats, file sharing up to 2GB, and cross-platform sync — are available at no cost, now and forever.

We believe secure communication is a fundamental right, not a premium feature. Tether is funded through optional premium cosmetic features (themes, sticker packs) and voluntary donations from our community. We will never show ads, sell your data, or gate essential features behind a paywall.

Tether sustains itself through three revenue streams, none of which compromise your privacy:

  • Premium Themes & Stickers: Optional cosmetic upgrades like exclusive theme packs, animated sticker sets, and custom chat wallpapers.
  • Tether Pro: A voluntary subscription ($2.99/month) that unlocks additional cloud storage, priority support, and early access to new features.
  • Community Donations: Many users choose to support Tether's mission directly through one-time or recurring donations.

Crucially, we will never sell user data, display advertisements, or monetize your attention. Our incentives are aligned with yours — we make money by making Tether better, not by exploiting your information.

Absolutely. Tether uses state-of-the-art end-to-end encryption based on the Signal Protocol, enhanced with our own improvements. Every message, voice note, call, image, video, and file is encrypted using AES-256-GCM — the same standard used by military and intelligence agencies.

Key security properties include:

  • Forward Secrecy: Even if a key is compromised, past messages cannot be decrypted.
  • Future Secrecy: Compromising a current key doesn't help decrypt future messages.
  • Zero-Knowledge: Our servers process only encrypted blobs — we cannot read your messages.
  • On-Device Keys: Encryption keys are generated and stored exclusively on your device.

Our encryption protocol is open source and has been independently audited three times by leading cybersecurity firms (CyberGuard International, NCC Group, and Trail of Bits), all confirming zero critical vulnerabilities.

Almost nothing. Tether is built on a zero-knowledge architecture. Here's exactly what we store:

  • Your Tether ID: A randomly generated username you create at signup. Not linked to your real name, phone number, or email.
  • Encrypted message blobs: Messages in transit are stored temporarily as encrypted data that we cannot decrypt. Once delivered, they are deleted from our servers.
  • Account creation date: When you created your account (no IP logging).

What we do NOT collect or store:

  • Your real name, phone number, or email address
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Your location or IP address
  • Message content, metadata, or who you talk to
  • Usage analytics, behavior tracking, or device fingerprints

For complete details, see our Privacy Policy.

No. Unlike most messaging apps, Tether does not require your phone number, email address, or any personally identifiable information to create an account.

To sign up, you simply:

  1. Download Tether from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Choose a unique Tether ID (your public username).
  3. Create a strong password.
  4. That's it — you're in. Start messaging immediately.

Your Tether ID is the only identifier associated with your account. It's not linked to any personal information, making your identity truly private from the start.

Disappearing messages give you complete control over the lifespan of your conversations. When enabled, messages are permanently deleted from all devices after a time period you choose:

  • 5 seconds
  • 30 seconds
  • 1 hour
  • 1 day
  • 7 days

The timer starts when the recipient reads the message. Once expired, the message is irrecoverably erased from both the sender's and recipient's devices, as well as any Tether servers. There is no backup, no archive, and no way to recover deleted messages.

You can enable disappearing messages on a per-conversation basis, and you can change the timer at any time. The setting applies to all new messages in that conversation — previous messages remain unaffected.

Tether supports group chats with up to 10,000 members. Every message in the group is end-to-end encrypted, regardless of group size.

Group features include:

  • Admin controls: Multiple admin levels with granular permissions.
  • Pinned messages: Keep important announcements at the top.
  • Polls: Create interactive polls with real-time results.
  • Threaded replies: Organized conversations within the group.
  • Shared media archive: Searchable gallery of all shared files.
  • Member roles: Assign custom roles with specific permissions.

For one-to-many broadcasting without replies, consider using Channels, which support unlimited subscribers.

Tether is available on all major platforms with full cross-platform sync:

  • Android: Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above
  • iOS: iOS 14 and above (iPhone, iPad)
  • Windows: Windows 10 and above
  • macOS: macOS 12 (Monterey) and above
  • Linux: Ubuntu 20.04+, Fedora 34+, Debian 11+ (AppImage and .deb available)
  • Web: Progressive Web App accessible from any modern browser

All platforms share the same feature set and maintain real-time sync. A message sent from your phone appears on your desktop instantly, and vice versa. Media, contacts, settings, and encryption keys are securely synced across all your devices.

Yes, absolutely. You have complete control over your data at all times. To delete your account:

  1. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  2. Confirm your identity with your password.
  3. Choose whether to also delete all your messages from recipients' devices (optional).
  4. Confirm deletion.

When you delete your account:

  • Your Tether ID is permanently released and cannot be reclaimed.
  • All your messages, media, and files on our servers are permanently erased.
  • Your encryption keys are destroyed.
  • You are removed from all group chats.
  • The process is irreversible — there is no recovery or "cooling off" period.

This process is completed within 24 hours. Since we operate on a zero-knowledge architecture, there's genuinely nothing left to delete beyond what's listed above.

Tether supports sharing files up to 2GB per file — one of the highest limits among messaging platforms.

Supported file types include:

  • Photos: JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF (including animated)
  • Videos: MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM
  • Audio: MP3, AAC, FLAC, OGG, WAV
  • Documents: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, TXT, and more
  • Archives: ZIP, RAR, 7Z, TAR.GZ
  • Any other file type — Tether doesn't restrict file formats.

All files are end-to-end encrypted before upload and stored in your personal encrypted cloud vault. Files are accessible from any device, at any time, without consuming local storage. Original quality is always preserved — we never compress your files without permission.
